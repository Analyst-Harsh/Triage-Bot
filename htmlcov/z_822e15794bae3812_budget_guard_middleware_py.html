11class BudgetGuardState(AgentState): 

12 """`AgentState` plus `run_meta` -- not middleware-private state (unlike 

13 e.g. `ModelCallLimitMiddleware`'s own `ModelCallLimitState`), but a key 

14 already owned by the parent graph (`TriageState`/`AgentLoopState`). 

15 LangGraph's subgraph nesting matches state by key name: since 

16 `AgentSubgraph.build_agent()`'s compiled graph is itself registered as 

17 the `"agent"` node inside `AgentSubgraph.compile()`'s own 

18 `StateGraph(AgentLoopState)`, declaring `run_meta` here is what makes 

19 the *current* value flow in from the parent state on every model turn, 

20 not just once at subgraph entry -- verified empirically (see this 

21 middleware's own test file for the nested-subgraph spike that confirms 

22 this before relying on it in production)."""

27class BudgetGuardMiddleware(AgentMiddleware[BudgetGuardState]): 

28 """Re-checks the run's cost budget before every in-loop model turn -- 

29 `AgentSubgraph.prepare_node`'s own `check_budget` call 

30 (`graph/nodes/utils/budget_guard.py`) only runs once, at subgraph entry, 

31 so it can't catch a multi-call Researcher/Drafter loop blowing through 

32 budget partway through. Projects cost including the in-flight 

33 trajectory (`estimate_trajectory_usage`, recomputed fresh each turn -- 

34 cheap, and avoids a second accumulator to keep checkpoint-synced with 

35 `RunMeta.estimated_cost_usd`) rather than waiting for the loop to finish 

36 and `assemble_node` to fold the real total back into `run_meta`. 

37 

38 Raises `BudgetExceededError` directly (rather than `exit_behavior="end"`, 

39 `ToolCallLimitMiddleware`'s softer style) -- a cost overrun is the one 

40 guardrail category this codebase treats as a hard abort, not a 

41 graceful degrade (see `graph/errors.py`'s docstring for why that's still 

42 safe: it flows through the same `handle_node_error` path as any other 

43 node exception, not a new propagation mechanism). 

44 """
